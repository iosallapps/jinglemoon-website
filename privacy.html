Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

JingleMoon is a Christmas app for families, made by Darius Cirjan. It has no ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking and no accounts.

What stays on your device

A child's first name (optional), letters, chat conversations during use, the chosen city, settings and daily use counts are kept only on the device. Letters are excluded from device backups. You can delete letters and the name in Settings, or delete everything by removing the app.

Santa's replies are written in the app or produced by Apple's on-device model, where available. Those messages do not leave the device.

The app does not ask for your location. It chooses a nearby city from the time zone setting of your device, and you can change it.

What is sent to others

Purchases. Purchases are made through Apple. The app uses RevenueCat, a service provider, to confirm what was bought. RevenueCat receives a random anonymous ID, the purchase history for that ID and basic app and device technical details needed for this. It is not used for advertising or tracking, and it contains no letter or chat text. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Maps. The close-up view of a city uses Apple Maps, which loads the map of the chosen city from Apple. See Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing. A certificate leaves the device only if a grown-up shares it, after passing the grown-ups check. Support. If you email us, we receive your email and what you write, and use them only to answer.

Children

The app is designed for families and asks for no personal information from children. The chat does not ask for names or contact details, and replies to worried messages point to a trusted grown-up. Purchases, outside links, sharing and Settings sit behind a grown-ups check. JingleMoon does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has sent us personal information, write to us and we will delete it.

Retention and your choices

We keep no copies of anything stored on your device. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to help you. You can ask for access to or deletion of data we hold at the address below. To remove purchase records linked to the anonymous ID, write to us with your request.
