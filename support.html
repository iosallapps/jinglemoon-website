Support

Write to us at iosallapps@icloud.com. Please tell us your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

Reporting a chat reply

If a reply from Santa looks wrong, use Report this reply in the chat, or write to us with what it said.

Restore a purchase

Open the app, go to Settings and choose Restore. The same Apple Account must be used.

Cancel a subscription

Open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose JingleMoon. Refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete letters and the name

In the app: Settings, then the delete row. Removing the app also deletes them.

Widgets and the Lock Screen countdown

Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen to add a widget. For the live Lock Screen countdown, tap the button on the countdown card and allow Live Activities when your device asks.
